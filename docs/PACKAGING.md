# Packaging imagery

The homepage uses two transparent WebP packshots based on the final packaging PDFs supplied on October 13, 2026. These are AI-generated product mockups of the supplied designs, not photographs of manufactured bags.

| Source artwork | Website asset |
| --- | --- |
| ZeusPitaCrisps-SEA SALT-printing.pdf | assets/zeus-aegean-sea-salt.webp |
| ZeusPitaCrisps-Greekherb_printing.pdf | assets/zeus-greek-garden-herbs.webp |

Both assets are 1122 × 1402 pixels with transparency. The same files are reused in the hero and product sections so the packaging stays consistent. The old hero and packshot assets have been removed. Source PDFs were not modified.

Generation used the built-in image-generation tool with a PNG rendering of each PDF's front panel as its reference and transparent background enabled. The finished PNGs were encoded as WebP at quality 88. Production guides and optional-feature overlays were excluded from the mockups.

## Aegean Sea Salt prompt

Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: isolated ecommerce product packshot for the Zeus Bakery website.
Input image 1: exact supplied front packaging artwork, supporting design reference to faithfully wrap onto the physical pouch.
Create a photorealistic upright 7-inch-wide by 9-inch-high resealable stand-up pouch of Zeus Bakery sourdough pita crisps using this exact artwork. One single pouch, front facing, nearly orthographic camera, whole pouch visible with 8 percent clear padding all around, portrait composition 4:5. Slightly rounded filled body, neatly heat-sealed navy top with a REAL die-cut hang hole, realistic matte pouch texture with tiny edge creases and soft studio lighting. Transparent background and soft subtle contact shadow. Preserve exact cream, navy and gold artwork, Zeus medallion, Greek key borders, mountain landscape and bowl of rectangular pita crisps. Flavor ribbon must be blue and read "AEGEAN SEA SALT". Preserve text "ZEUS BAKERY", "SOURDOUGH", "PITA CRISPS", "HAND-BAKED IN COLORADO", "VEGAN", "50+ YEAR OLD SOURDOUGH STARTER", "SEED-OIL FREE", "Net Wt. 5.7 oz (162 g)". Remove all printer-only marks: purple dashed safe-area guides, gold cut-tolerance edge bars, thin cut guide rules, and blue translucent zipper/hang-hole overlays. The zipper zone above ZEUS BAKERY is clean cream, not blue-gray. Do not redesign the packaging or invent claims. No loose food, no props, no background, no additional bags or floating text. Make the finished pack beautiful, accurate, legible and premium.
