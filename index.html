02 / OUR STORY

Greek roots.
Colorado heart.

For the Kircos family, bread has always brought people together.

Our story began in Greece and crossed the ocean to America, where our family built a Detroit bakery known for its soft, flavorful Greek pita.

Years later, after moving to Colorado, Caroline and David Kircos missed the bread they grew up with. Nothing in the local stores tasted quite like the family recipe. So they started Zeus Bakery.

Today, we’re carrying that tradition forward—from familiar, warm Greek pita to a new kind of sourdough crunch. The same family spirit, with a little more Colorado in every bite.

From our family to your table.
