A FAMILY RECIPE. A NEW CHAPTER.

02 / OUR STORY

Greek roots.
Colorado heart.

We're Caroline and David Kircos.

David's family has been baking pita for generations, first in a small village in Greece and later in Detroit, where they opened a bakery in the 1950s. Caroline's family brought something else to the table: a sourdough starter her grandmother was given by a neighbor in the 1960s. It's been part of the family ever since.

When we started baking pita at home, we used his family's recipe with her family's starter, and we wondered what else it could do. The answer was the pita chip. Most come out of a factory bland, brittle, and made with seed oils. We thought the pita chip deserved better.

So we make our pita crisps by hand with that same starter, heritage grains grown and milled here in Colorado, extra virgin olive oil, and sea salt. Zeus Bakery pita crisps are a new take on something old, and we hope they become a staple at your table too.

From our family to your table.
